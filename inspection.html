About This Division

Integrity at the Core of Every Reading

Stock discrepancies are expensive and hard to catch after the fact. JDL Core exists to catch them before they become someone's loss — verifying quantities, monitoring collateral, and supervising loading and discharge so operators, lenders, and traders can make decisions on numbers that actually hold up.

  • Ten inspection and verification services across the value chain
  • A consistent six-step process from request to signed report

Our Services

Ten Ways We Keep Stock Numbers Honest

From a single tank reading to a full inventory audit, each service follows the same documented, independent process.

Stock Monitoring

Ongoing tracking of inventory levels against expected movement, flagging deviations early.

Collateral Verification

Independent confirmation of pledged stock quantity and condition for lenders and financiers.

Tank & Depot Inspections

On-site inspection of tanks and depot facilities, covering condition, calibration, and safety.

Quantity Verification

Precise measurement (GOV, GSV, metric tonnes) to confirm quantities match what's claimed.

Reconciliation & Exception Reporting

Comparing expected vs. actual stock and reporting exceptions with supporting evidence.

Loading & Discharge Supervision

On-site oversight during loading and discharge to confirm quantities transferred are accurate.

Inventory Audit Support

Structured audit assistance for periodic or ad-hoc inventory reviews.

Loss & Discrepancy Investigation

Root-cause investigation when reported and physical stock don't match.

Documentation & Reporting

Clear, standardized reports — including Certificates of Quantity — built to hold up under scrutiny.

Stock Control Advisory

Practical recommendations for tightening stock control processes based on what we find in the field.

How It Works

Request to Report, in Six Steps

The same structured process for every job, so a report means the same thing every time.

1

Request

Client submits an inspection request with job details and site information.

2

Assignment

Operations reviews the request and assigns a qualified inspector.

3

Inspection

The inspector performs the on-site work and captures field data and documentation.

4

Verification

Findings are checked and reconciled against expected figures before write-up.

5

Review

Operations reviews the report for accuracy and completeness before it's finalized.

6
Final Step

Report

Client receives the signed report — including a Certificate of Quantity where applicable.
